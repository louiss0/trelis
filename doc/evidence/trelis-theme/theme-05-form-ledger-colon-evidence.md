  `lib/prompty.dart`.
- `_StructureSpine` (internal, `lib/src/text_input.dart`) gained the three
  grammars through the same seam as the prefix, enclosure and heading paths:
  - `heading()` numbers a numbered structure, draws the grammar's rule under
    the heading at the measured stage width in the palette's border role, and
    drops the decoration or the number when the columns cannot hold them.
  - `write(..., event:)` and `_writeHeadedBody(..., event:)` spell a typed
    event label at the reference column and keep the component's own words

    `eventPrefixes` writes exactly the bytes it wrote before the kind existed.
  - `hint()` uses the grammar's `hintLead`, so the form hint sits under the
    edged field, the ledger hint is a `note:` line and the diagnostic hint is a

    the first entry of a stage identity allocates the next number, a revisit
    reuses it, and `reset()` (repaint, resize, next page, next field) never

- The component-owned text that is answer data, a literal path, a base path, a

## Structure grammar check

`test/theme_form_ledger_colon_framing_test.dart` runs every applicable
interactive component (all 34) through each of the three structures in both
interaction modes, plus the four feedback components per structure (and both
modes for the three live ones), and asserts:

- **Form** — the first line is the component's own label spelled in capitals and
  the rule follows the heading block and spans the stage width; body rows carry
  the left edge (`│ ` Unicode, `| ` ASCII) and the hint sits below the edged
  field with its own indent; a wrapped heading continues under that indent
  instead of pretending to be a field row.
- **Ledger** — the first line is `01 / <heading>`, the dotted rule (`·`
  Unicode, `.` ASCII) follows the heading block, body rows are indented three
  cells and the compact hint is a `   note: ` line.
- **Colon** — the first line is an `ask:` heading, the body is indented two
  cells, the compact hint is a `hint:` line and no rule is drawn; the typed
  labels `state:`, `warning:` and `result:` appear at the reference column with
  the component's own words after them. A `Slider` refusal (`Required: enter a
  non-empty value and submit again.`) and a `MaskedInput` refusal (`Required
  slots are incomplete.`) are asserted as `warning:` rows, so the typed-event
  wiring is checked on two components that do not write through the shared
  editor path.
- No line of any run contains enclosure chrome (`┌ ┐ └ ┘ ╭ ╮ ╰ ╯ ╔ ╗ ╚ ╝ ║ ├`),
  so none of the three falls back to a framed or undecorated presentation.
- No line exceeds the declared columns, a 200-column profile still stays inside
  the 120-cell prompt width cap, and 24 columns keep every line inside 24.
- Above the 80-cell rule cap the stage does not widen: at 80 and at 120 columns
  a form and a ledger stage render the same lines, the rule is drawn at
  `structureRuleWidth(structure, columns)` (80) and no heading or body row is
  wider than the rule that spans it.
- At 4 columns the decoration is simplified — the diagnostic marker and the
  ledger note marker give way rather than overflowing — while the structure's
  own body marker stays in the output, so the structure is never silently
  replaced. At 8 columns the rule, the number and the edge all still fit.
- Rich output uses the declared Unicode vocabulary (`─`, `│`, `·`) and an
  `automatic` glyph profile falls back to the portable ASCII counterpart; the
  diagnostic grammar has exactly one ASCII vocabulary under every profile (the
  Unicode and ASCII lookups are the same object).
- A palette `foreground` styles the stage while the accepted value is
  unchanged, and a palette `border` styles the rule as chrome.
- The answer data is never case-transformed (`Ada Lovelace` stays `Ada
  Lovelace` under a capitalized form heading), a literal `/Help` in validation
  text is never re-cased, a protected message keeps its `\u{0007}` spelling, a
  masked password is never echoed, an unavailable choice keeps its reason and a
  reported outcome stays in the stage.
- Stage help keeps the structure grammar.
- Standalone feedback gains the structure's live marker without a frame for
  `Text`, `Progress`, `Spinner` and `MultiSpinner` in both modes.
- `TextInput`, `Select`, `Confirm` and `Password` produce the same outcome for
  `plain`, `form`, `ledger` and `colon` in both modes, and the reference
  presentation still emits no decoration.

### Ledger numbering scope

Numbering is display sequencing only, and it is scoped to one invocation:

- a ledger stage always starts at `01`;
- the number is allocated the first time a stage identity (the heading the
  component owns) is rendered and reused for every repaint, resize, page, next
  field and resumed help screen of that identity — the framing test drives five
  rich repaints of one `Select` and four field stages of one `TimeInput` and
  gets `01` every time;
- a linear `/help` request does not allocate a number, and the resumed stage
  keeps `01`;
- every invocation starts a fresh sequence: a second prompt in the same process
  and two prompts running concurrently both show `01`;
- the factory namespaces hold no numbering state, so construction purity is the
  reason reuse and concurrency are safe: the map lives on the stage writer of
  the invocation that renders the stage;
- cross-prompt flow numbering and a caller-supplied step interface remain
  deferred by the resolved design decision, so a single prompt is a single
  numbered stage.

### Diagnostic labels are display labels

`ask:`, `hint:`, `state:`, `warning:` and `result:` are display prefixes only.
A heading's spelling is not a command: `ask:/help` typed as an answer is
accepted verbatim as text while a bare `/help` remains the command, and a
literal `\/help` in validation text keeps its spelling. The typed labels never
re-case or reword the semantic content that follows them.

## Limitations and remaining work

- A single prompt is one meaningful task stage, so a ledger prompt displays
  `01`. The sequencing machinery is per identity and per invocation: a component
  that presents a second heading inside one invocation would display `02` and
  keep it on every revisit, but no current component does, so the multi-stage
  case is exercised by the vocabulary helpers rather than by a component flow.
  Cross-prompt numbering is explicitly deferred by the resolved design decision.
- The atlas form pads its label to a fixed column (`PROJECT NAME      FIELD`).
  That is a specimen constant, not a layout rule, so this implementation spells
  the label in capitals and draws the rule at the measured width instead of
  padding to a fixed column.
- The atlas ledger indents its body and marks the focused row with `> `; this
  implementation indents the body and keeps the shared numbered, focus-marked
  choice rows every structure already had. The number is the ledger's own
  decoration; the rows are not renumbered.
- The diagnostic grammar is ASCII in every profile, exactly as the console
  grammar is: `ask:`, `hint:`, `state:`, `warning:`, `result:` and the `::`
  body marker are the structure, so no glyph profile changes them.
- A typed label is dropped rather than overflowed when the columns cannot hold
  it (for example `hint:` below six columns). The event keeps its words; the
  label returns as soon as the width allows it.
- `warning:` rows written through the compact-hint path (for example
  `ColorInput`'s preview notice and `DurationInput`'s interpretation notice)
  keep the `hint:` label, because those components close the stage with that
  row rather than writing an event row.
- A few component-local refusals are still body rows, because they are raised
  outside the shared write seams typed here: `Autocomplete`'s
  "Text exceeds maximum length", "Suggestions unavailable" and "Candidate
  exceeds maximum length" notices, `SearchSelect`'s searching, unavailable and
  paste refusals, and the dedicated editor loop's raw paste refusals. They keep
  the component's own words under `ColonTheme` and read as indented body rows
  rather than `warning:` rows; typing them is a change in those components, not
  in this ticket's surfaces.
- A rich repaint redraws the heading, the rule and the body rows for each
  presentation, the same way a rail repeats its spine and a frame redraws its
  rules. The compact hint is written once per stage for the choice families and
  re-written per field stage for `DateInput`/`TimeInput`.
- No native-terminal visual qualification was performed: every result here is
  scripted output, not a screenshot or a manual terminal review. Windows native
  and GUI probe evidence for the theming families remains a separate ticket.

## Conclusion

The `FormTheme`, `LedgerTheme` and `ColonTheme` factory namespaces, the
structured-label vocabulary and its composition helpers, and the three grammars
across every applicable interactive and feedback render path are implemented,
verified by 3069 passing tests, and committed. The plain, rail, branch, box,
round, double, minimal, ascii, chevron and bracket presentations keep the bytes
they wrote before this ticket, with one deliberate exception at the very narrow
end: at one or two columns a marker that cannot fit is dropped instead of
overflowing the declared width, so the ascii console's `--` and the ascii
chevron's `->` hint marker give way at two columns and the heading marker at
one. Those runs are one or two cells shorter than they used to be instead of
one or two cells too wide; nothing changes above three columns. The ledger of
protected content and outcomes is unchanged, accepted values, choice
references, input bindings and completion outcomes are identical to the
reference presentation, and the ticket is **resolved** with the worktree ready
for merger.
